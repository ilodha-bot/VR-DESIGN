Project Architectural Overview

Architectural Vision

The Kundam Market redevelopment integrates traditional bazaar spatial typologies with contemporary passive climate design. It provides structured stalls for over 150 local vendors, replacing informal corrugated sheds with permanent, environmentally responsive timber and earthen architecture.

🌤 Passive Ventilation

High-pitched roof shapes act as thermal chimneys, drawing cool air across open-air market walkways and discharging hot air continuously.

💧 Rainwater Harvesting

Integrated butterfly gutters lead into sub-surface cisterns storing up to 120,000 liters for stall sanitation and courtyard irrigation.

🌳 Microclimate Central Courtyard

Centered around preserved mature Banyan trees, creating shaded community gathering steps and lowering ambient temperatures by 3-4°C.

☀️ Solar Energy Canopy

Lightweight translucent solar panels line high roofs, generating clean power while filtering daylight to prevent produce spoilage.
