PURNA MARKET REDEVELOPMENT

Welcome to the virtual design review walkthrough for the proposed Purna Market (Kundam Market) Redevelopment. This platform provides an immersive spatial experience designed for faculty jurors, practicing architects, municipal clients, and visitors to inspect spatial organization, material selections, environmental response, and zoning logic.

The redevelopment replaces informal vendor sprawl with structured, ventilated market blocks integrated around passive cooling courtyards. Built using Compressed Stabilized Earth Blocks (CSEB), local bamboo shading canopies, and terracotta tile roofing, the design prioritizes community accessibility, vendor ergonomics, and natural daylighting.

Total Site Area 12,400 sq.m
Functional Zones 12 Market Sectors
Cooling Strategy Passive Stack & Courtyard
