ORBITLeft-click and drag to rotate around the building.
ZOOMUse the mouse wheel or pinch gesture.
PANRight-click and drag to shift the view.
INSPECTSelect a stall, stair, court or hall to view its use.
WALKEnter VR mode and use WASD or the on-screen directional pad.
FLOORSChoose Ground, First, Second, Third or Fourth Floor. The selected level opens as an isolated 3D floor view.
MARKET JOURNEYUse the Market Journey control to move through the street entrance, flower stalls, local produce, weighing counter and courtyard.
SHOPPINGInspect stalls for local market stories. In VR, point at fruit or vegetables and hold the controller trigger to pick them up.
Follow MARKET JOURNEY for a guided visit. People move between stalls, and the two English–Telugu information boards introduce the market and its local produce. VR / AR headset support depends on browser, device and secure-context availability; a simulated mode remains available on desktop and mobile.
